1. Executive Summary: Moving From Reactive Detection to Predictive Mitigation

Traditional industrial coal stockpile safety operates reactively: sprinklers or water suppression systems are triggered only after surface smoke or elevated surface temperatures are detected. However, because coal is an exceptional thermal insulator, by the time surface sensors register heat (>60°C), the subterranean core has already escalated to critical thermal runaway (>100°C - 200°C).

The Innovation in CoalSafe-MMTS v2:

  • Sliding 1-Hour Temporal Window: Consumes the past 60 minutes [t-60m ... t] of multimodal sensor history (10 gas and physical dynamics channels + 50×50 spatial thermal images).
  • +1-Hour Horizon Future Forecasting (t+60 min): Explicitly forecasts the future risk score (±1h Risk) and the internal sub-surface core temperature (Core Temp °C) 60 minutes into the future.
  • Closed-Loop Pre-emptive Early Mitigation: If the +1h future forecasted risk crosses the actionable threshold (≥ 60%) with validated cross-modal consensus, early cooling is activated immediately at time t. This caps core temperatures at 38.1°C, averting thermal runaway entirely and providing a massive lead-time head-start advantage.
  • Cross-Modal Conflict Gating: Detects single-sensor anomalies (e.g. electrical CO spikes, solar glare on thermal cameras) and suppresses false alarms (|Δ| > 25%).

2. Multi-Horizon Neural Network Architecture (CoalSafe-MMTS)

The network features a unified deep multimodal pipeline with 653,329 trainable parameters:

1. Temporal Time-Series Branch (Sensors)

Consumes sequence X_tab (batch × 6 steps × 10 channels) through a 2-layer Bi-directional GRU (hidden dim = 64) equipped with Temporal Self-Attention. Produces 128-d temporal latent vector h_tab.

2. Spatial Thermal Image Branch (Infrared Camera)

Consumes 50×50×3 thermal heatmap images through a 3-stage 2D-CNN (32 → 64 → 128 filters) with LeakyReLU and Adaptive Average Pooling. Produces 128-d spatial latent vector h_img.

3. Gated Bilinear Cross-Modal Fusion

Calculates adaptive modality reliability weight g = σ(W_g [h_tab; h_img]) and synthesizes multimodal representation h_fused in R^256.

4. Multi-Horizon & Internal State Prediction Heads
  • tab_head & img_head: Auxiliary present-moment modality estimations.
  • fused_reg_head: Present-moment unified risk score (y_now ∈ [0, 100%]).
  • forecast_60m_head: +1-Hour Future Risk Forecaster (y_60m ∈ [0, 100%]).
  • forecast_temp_core_60m_head: +1-Hour Internal Core Temperature Forecaster (T_core_60m in °C).
  • forecast_class_60m_head: 5-Class Future Risk Classifier (NORMAL, LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, CRITICAL).

7. Professor / Viva Examination Defense Cheatsheet

Q1: Why is future forecasting (t+60 min) essential instead of just detecting current risk (t)?

Answer: Coal spontaneous combustion has significant thermal inertia. By the time surface temperature rises noticeably, deep core oxidation is already well advanced. Forecasting +1h ahead allows the system to trigger early cooling while the core is still cool (38.1°C), completely preventing runaway rather than reacting to an active fire.

Q2: How does the model forecast future core temperature without physical core probes?

Answer: Internal core temperature is unmeasurable directly in large stockpiles. The model's Bi-GRU with Temporal Attention learns the thermodynamic transfer function from surface gas accumulation rates (ΔCO, ΔCO2, heating rate) and surface thermal gradients over a 1-hour sliding window to infer and forecast sub-surface core temperature.

Q3: How do you prevent false activations when a sensor malfunctions?

Answer: The Conflict Gating module computes modality disagreement |y_tab - y_img|. If a single sensor spikes (e.g. CO glitch or camera lens flare), the disagreement exceeds 25%, flagging is_valid = False and suppressing spray activation until human verification.
